📖 Pattern Definitions & Theory

🏠 Summary 📈 Detailed Analyzer

Understanding the underlying psychology of each candlestick pattern is critical for successful trading. Below is a theoretical guide detailing the strength rankings (1 = Weak, 5 = Extremely Strong), intended outcomes, and potential failure scenarios of all supported patterns.

Pattern Name Type Strength Meaning & Technical Info Possible Outcomes If Pattern Fails
Morning Star (5) Bullish Level 5 A robust 3-candle bottom reversal. Forms during a downtrend. Consists of a long red candle, a small-bodied middle candle (indecision), and a strong green candle closing well into the first red body. High probability of a strong trend reversal to the upside. Often marks the absolute bottom of a swing. If price breaks below the low of the middle candle, the reversal is invalidated, signaling aggressive downside continuation.
Evening Star (5) Bearish Level 5 A robust 3-candle top reversal. Forms during an uptrend. Consists of a long green candle, a small-bodied middle candle, and a strong red candle closing into the first green body. High probability of a strong trend reversal to the downside. Resistance is firmly established. If price breaks above the high of the middle candle, bears have failed, leading to a massive short-squeeze upwards.
Bullish Engulfing (4) Bullish Level 4 A 2-candle bottom reversal. A small red candle is completely engulfed by a massive green candle, showing a total shift in momentum from sellers to buyers. Immediate upside momentum. Buyers have taken full control and will likely push higher. If the low of the green engulfing candle is broken, the bulls are trapped and a harsh selloff will follow.
Bearish Engulfing (4) Bearish Level 4 A 2-candle top reversal. A small green candle is entirely engulfed by a massive red candle, indicating sellers have completely overwhelmed buyers. Immediate downside momentum. Sellers are heavily defending resistance. If the high of the red engulfing candle is breached, resistance breaks, usually causing a violent leg up.
Piercing Line (4) Bullish Level 4 A 2-candle bottom reversal. A long red candle is followed by a green candle that gaps down but closes above the 50% midpoint of the red candle. Strong bullish reversal, though slightly less aggressive than an engulfing. Failure occurs if price drops below the green candle's low, signaling bears are still in total control.
Dark Cloud Cover (4) Bearish Level 4 A 2-candle top reversal. A long green candle is followed by a red candle that gaps up but closes below the 50% midpoint of the green candle. Strong bearish reversal, confirming overhead supply and rejection of higher prices. If buyers push price above the high of the red candle, the bearish thesis is voided.
Marubozu Bullish (4) Bullish Level 4 A 1-candle momentum pattern. A massive green body with virtually no upper or lower wicks. Buyers controlled the price from open to close. Extremely strong continuation or breakout signal. Momentum is firmly upwards. If the next candle immediately reverses and breaks the low, it signifies a devastating "bull trap".
Marubozu Bearish (4) Bearish Level 4 A 1-candle momentum pattern. A massive red body with virtually no wicks. Sellers controlled the price entirely. Extremely strong continuation or breakdown signal. Momentum is firmly downwards. If the next candle immediately rallies above the high, it indicates a severe "bear trap".
Hammer (3) Bullish Level 3 A 1-candle reversal. Small body with a long lower wick (at least 2x the body). Shows aggressive buying rejection at lower prices. Moderate upside potential. Often requires the next candle to close higher for confirmation. If the lower wick is broken, it shows buyers have given up, leading to a steep drop.
Shooting Star (3) Bearish Level 3 A 1-candle reversal. Small body with a long upper wick. Shows aggressive selling rejection at higher prices. Moderate downside potential. Often requires the next candle to close lower for confirmation. If the upper wick is broken, sellers are exhausted and price will surge higher.
Bullish Harami (2) Bullish Level 2 A 2-candle "inside bar" pattern. A large red candle followed by a small green candle completely contained within the red body. Indicates selling pressure has paused. Weak reversal signal; acts more as an early warning. If price breaks the low of the first red candle, the downtrend effortlessly continues.
Bearish Harami (2) Bearish Level 2 A 2-candle "inside bar" pattern. A large green candle followed by a small red candle completely contained within the green body. Indicates buying pressure has paused. Weak reversal signal; requires severe confirmation. If price breaks the high of the first green candle, the uptrend easily resumes.
Inverted Hammer (2) Bullish Level 2 A 1-candle reversal found at the bottom of a downtrend. Small body with a long upper wick. Weak reversal. Indicates buyers attempted to step in but were pushed back down. Requires a strong green candle next. Very prone to failure if the next candle is red, resulting in immediate continuation of the downtrend.
Hanging Man (2) Bearish Level 2 A 1-candle reversal found at the top of an uptrend. Small body with a long lower wick. Weak reversal. Indicates sellers tried to push price down but buyers rescued it. Only bearish if the *next* candle drops. Highly prone to failure. If the next candle is green, the uptrend continues strongly.
Doji (1) Neutral Level 1 A 1-candle pattern where the open and close are virtually identical, creating a cross shape. Pure indecision. Buyers and sellers are in perfect equilibrium. The trend will pause. A Doji cannot "fail" as it has no inherent direction, but a breakout of its high/low dictates the next move.
